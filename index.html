The Problem

The "I Forgot!" Problem

Students and young professionals manage assignments, exams, meetings, appointments and deadlines. When everything comes with reminders and notifications, it becomes difficult to know what needs attention first.

📚

Too Many Tasks

Multiple deadlines compete for attention at once, making it hard to know where to start.

🔔

Too Many Notifications

Constant reminders from apps, emails, and messages can become paralyzing instead of helpful.

❓

No Clear Priority

Users don't always know what to do first when everything feels equally important.

The Solution

Meet FokusMe

FokusMe turns a long and confusing task list into a simple daily focus system. Three priorities, three tasks, one clear direction.

🔴

Urgent

Needs immediate attention. Do these today.

🟡

Important

Needs to be completed soon. Plan for these next.

🟢

Can Wait

Can be completed later. Don't stress about these today.

📌 Today's Top 3

FokusMe highlights the three tasks that deserve your attention today — automatically, based on priority and deadline.

How It Works

Five steps to a focused day

From adding your first task to tracking your progress, FokusMe keeps it simple.

1

Add a Task

Enter what you need to do — an assignment, meeting or deadline.

2

Set Date & Time

Choose when it needs to be completed.

3

Select Priority

Choose Urgent, Important, or Can Wait.

4

Focus on Top 3

FokusMe highlights your three most important tasks automatically.

5

Complete & Track

Mark tasks done and watch your progress grow.
